          How it works
          1. Drop a video from this device. It is copied into Flicker's work folder on this machine.
          2. Set the start and end on the rail, pick a tool, and render.
          3. Save the result. Open videos and renders are cleared from the work folder on their own after a while.
          Privacy

          Everything runs on this machine with ffmpeg. There is no account, no analytics and no cloud. Two optional lookups reach the internet, and only when you use them: the lyrics lookup sends an artist and song title, and link import (off unless switched on in config.json) fetches the link you paste.
