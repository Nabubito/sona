Library folders

Point Reel at one or more folders on this computer. Add as many drives and paths as you like; Reel merges them into one library. Save, then rescan.

Profiles

Each profile is its own sign in with separate favorites, playlists and history. Add one for another person; they unlock with their own 4 digit code on the normal lock screen.

Guest access

Share temporary access. A guest code works on the normal lock screen and expires on its own; the guest is signed out the moment it lapses or you revoke it.
