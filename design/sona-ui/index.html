Sona UI

Design system

One home, three rooms.

Warm charcoal and ember gold, Cormorant Garamond for the voice and Jost for the work. This page renders every token and component straight from tokens.css and components.css, so what you see here is exactly what Kin, Reel and Attic ship.

Color

Semantic tokens, resolved live for the current theme. Every text pair clears WCAG AA (4.5:1) in both dark and light.

Type

Fluid sizes that scale from a 360px phone to a 1440px desktop with clamp(). Display in Cormorant Garamond, everything else in Jost.

Motion

Short, springy and quiet. With reduced motion switched on in the OS, every duration collapses to 1ms and the ambient glows stop.

Icons

One sprite, icons.svg. Stroke icons inherit color from the text around them.

Buttons

Every control is at least 44 by 44 pixels, including the small icon buttons, which keep an invisible hit area.

Inputs

Inputs never drop below 16px, so phones do not zoom on focus.
