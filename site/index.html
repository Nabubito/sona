The suite

Five apps. One roof.

Each one is a small program on your computer. Run one, or run all five. They share one look, one lock screen and one rule: your things stay yours.

Your people

Kin

Instead of WhatsApp and FaceTime

Private calls and chat with the people you love. Voice and video go straight from device to device, and messages stay on the server in your home. No phone number handed to a company, no one in the middle.

  • Voice and video calls, device to device
  • Messages, voice notes and scheduled messages
  • Installs to your home screen like any app

Your music and film

Reel

Instead of Netflix and Spotify

Your own media, streamed from your own files. Point Reel at a folder and it sorts everything into a library that plays on any screen you own. No ads, no rent, no one logging what you watch.

  • Music and video from the files you already have
  • Albums, artists, genres, playlists and favorites
  • Plays on your phone, laptop or TV

Your photos

Attic

Instead of iCloud Photos and Google Photos

A photo and video vault that lives in a plain folder on your computer. Shoot from any phone's browser and every frame lands at home. Delete Sona tomorrow and your photos are still right there.

  • Camera, library and favorites from any device
  • Normal images in normal folders
  • No storage plan to upgrade, ever

Your file workshop

Forge

Instead of online converters, PowerISO and WinRAR

Drop in a video, an archive or a disc image and Forge shows you what it can do. Turn a clip into a GIF, trim it, shrink it for email, convert it, open or build a zip or 7z, browse a disc image. No upload, no size caps, no watermark.

  • Video to GIF, trim, shrink, convert, pull out the audio
  • Open and build zip and 7z archives
  • Browse and extract disc images, mount them on Windows

Your video studio

Flicker

Instead of CapCut and online editors

A video effects studio made for your phone and run by your PC. Cut a clip, loop it into a GIF, reframe it for vertical feeds, burn in captions, or pin words to something and watch them follow it through the shot.

  • Cut, GIF and reframe to 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and more
  • Your own words or captions, burned in
  • Text that follows what moves

How it stays private

Your PC is the cloud now.

Not a privacy policy you have to trust. The data physically never leaves your house, and you can read the code to check.

Your PC at home

  • Runs on your PC

    Every app is a small program on a computer you already own. Your files stay in plain folders on your own disk.

  • Reach it from your phone

    Your phone and your PC join your own private mesh VPN, end to end encrypted. Nothing is opened to the public internet.

  • No cloud account

    No sign up, no subscription, no company server holding your life. Each app opens with a passcode only you know.

  • No tracking

    No telemetry, no analytics, no phone home. Not in the apps, and not on this page either.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need to be technical?

Today, a little. You install Node.js and start each app with one command, and the guide walks you through it. A one click installer is on the way: join the waitlist above and we will tell you when it is ready.

What does it cost?

The apps are free and open source, and they stay that way. A paid Sona Pro with the one click installer and priority support is planned to keep the project going.

What computer do I need?

Any Windows, macOS or Linux computer that can stay on while you want to use it. A few extras ask for more: mounting disc images in Forge is Windows only, and text follow in Flicker wants an NVIDIA graphics card.

How do I reach it when I am away from home?

Install a private mesh VPN, such as Tailscale or any WireGuard mesh, on your PC and on your phone. They meet on your own encrypted network, so your phone reaches your apps from anywhere with nothing exposed to the internet.

What happens to my things if I stop using Sona?

Nothing. Your music, films and photos are plain files in plain folders on your own disk. Uninstall Sona and they are exactly where you left them.

Does this website track me?

No. No cookies, no analytics, no fonts or scripts from anyone else. This page only talks to sona.casa, and your email is used for one message about the installer.
